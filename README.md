# Adventure card game

ASCII travel on one 25×25 plains zone. Rules are in [NOTES.md](NOTES.md).

Swipe, the arrow keys, or the on-screen arrows move one square. Open the menu for your deck, your player card, and settings. Step on `V` to trade. Step on `R` to search the ruin. Step on `S` to fight the slime. Wolves can meet you on open ground.
