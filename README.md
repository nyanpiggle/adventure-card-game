# Adventure card game

ASCII travel on one 25×25 plains zone. Rules are in [NOTES.md](NOTES.md).

Swipe, the arrow keys, or the on-screen arrows move one square. Open the menu for your deck, your player card, and settings. Step on `V` to trade or rest at the inn. Step on `R` to enter the ruin. Step on `S` to fight the slime. Wolves can meet you on open ground. At night, and in the ruin, they come as a pair: one hitbox, an empty column between them, and a long shape can cut both. In a fight, drag a card's shape onto the creature.
